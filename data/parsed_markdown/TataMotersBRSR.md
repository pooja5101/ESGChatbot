



# Executive Summary: Tata Motors BRSR FY 2024-25

## Company Overview & Reporting Boundary
* **Entity Covered:** The report consolidates data for Tata Motors Limited (TML), Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited (TMPVL), and Tata Passenger Electric Mobility Limited (TPEML).
* **Financial Scope:** The combined turnover for these entities in FY 2024-25 was ₹1,31,421 crore.
* **Materiality:** For the first time, Tata Motors adopted a "materiality assessment" to evaluate ESG issues that are financially important to the business and socially impactful to stakeholders.

## Environmental Performance (Principle 6)
* **Climate & Emissions:** Tata Motors is committed to achieving Net Zero Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions by 2045 for the Commercial Vehicle business and by 2040 for the Passenger Vehicle business.
* **Renewable Energy:** The company is a signatory to Climate Group RE100, aiming for 100% renewable electricity in its operations by 2030. The combined entities consumed 6,97,539 GJ of energy from renewable sources in FY 2024-25.
    * GHG footprint for the combined entities: Total Scope 1 emissions were 70,746 metric tonnes of CO2 equivalent, Scope 2 emissions were 2,14,288 metric tonnes of CO2, and Scope 3 emissions totaled 162,302,158 metric tonnes of CO2.
* **Water Management:** The company aims at a 'Water Positive' status by 2030. Several manufacturing plants are currently certified as 'Water Positive' and operate as Zero Liquid Discharge facilities.
* **Waste & Circularity:** Tata Motors is committed to achieving "Zero Waste to Landfill" by 2030 across operations. To handle end-of-life vehicles, the company has expanded its "Re.Wi.Re. - Recycle with Respect" franchise facilities to safely dismantle and recycle aged vehicles.
* **Social Performance & Workforce (Principle 3, 5 & 8)**
    * **Workforce Demographics:** The combined workforce consists of 12,956 employees and 45,486 workers.
    * **Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI):** The company has set a goal to have 25% women in leadership roles by 2024. Tata Motors is also implementing Diversity pilots for the LGBQT+ community and differently-abled candidates.
    * **Employee Well-Being:** 100% of permanent employees are covered by health insurance, accident insurance, and maternity/paternity benefits.
    * **Occupational Health & Safety:** The company adheres to an ISO 45001-aligned safety management system and reported a combined 67 total recordable injuries for employees and 25 for workers, with 1 worker fatality in FY24.
* **Human Rights (Principle 1)**
    * **Human Rights:** The company ensures strict compliance against child labor and forced labor, and 22 POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) complaints were filed during the year.
    * **Community & CSR:** CSR initiatives reached 14,88,301 beneficiaries, with 100% belonging to vulnerable and marginalized groups.
* **Governance & Business Ethics (Principle 1 & 7)**
    * **Ethical Framework:** Governance is guided by the Tata Code of Conduct and strict Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption policies.
    * **Grievance Mechanisms:** The company provides a Whistleblower Policy and an Ethics Helpline (available in multiple languages for anonymous reporting of violations.
    * **No penalties or fines were imposed by regulators regarding corruption or conflicts of interest during the financial year.**
* **Sustainable Sourcing & Supply Chain (Principle 2 & 9)**
    * **Product Innovation & Supply Chain:** Tata Motors enforces a Supplier Code of Conduct and evaluates suppliers on ESG parameters.
* **R&D Investments:** The company dedicated 41% of its total R&D expenditure on technologies that improve the environmental and social impacts of its products.
* **Data Privacy:** Cyber security is covered by the Risk Management Committee, and the company adheres to ISO 2001 standards to protect customer data. No data breaches involving customer information were reported in FY 2024-25.
